clickSiege privacy policy

fun little game made for hackclub by lolodotzip

what data is stored ON-DEVICE

on your device, this project stores data, also known as browser cookies data, saved in the common localStorage. this includes the following:

* if you passed first initialization

* your username (if you set one)

* your 5 last recent scores

* your 5 best recent scores

data sent to the Internet

by default, if you didn't agree to it (by opting in to the leaderboard and setting a username), no data is sent. however, if you did opt-in, we collect the following:

* your username

* the score you performed

* the timestamp recorded of your score (in your local time).

like every website, we may also collect your IP address and be able to cross-track it to know which user's IP address is which. this will only be done as a last-resort solution to exploiters (spamming and griefing the gdocs), to block you access from cf.

i opted in, but i want to opt out now!!!

to do such, either erase your browser data storage to reset the game, or go to the localStorage (devtools > Application), and erase the "username" key, and the "firstinit" key. this will essentially reset the game, allowing you to opt-out.
